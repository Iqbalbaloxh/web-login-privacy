Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Operator: Mr Aftab · Contact: supprot@legendsmmpanel.com

1. Request deletion from the operator

Email supprot@legendsmmpanel.com with subject web login — Data Deletion Request. State what data you believe the developer holds and how to identify the records. Do not send passwords, session cookies or access tokens. We will review your request and reply in accordance with applicable privacy law. If no developer-hosted copy exists, we will explain that instead of claiming that we removed local files from your computer.

2. Delete local files

  1. Close the web login / extractor application.
  2. Open its working folder on your computer.
  3. Delete .token.txt and .cookie.txt if present. These contain sensitive authentication material in the described legacy version.
  4. Delete exported link TXT files inside FB_VAULT_LINKS and any target input files you no longer need.
  5. Empty the Recycle Bin if appropriate, and remove any copies held in OneDrive, backups, external drives or other synchronized locations.

For a safer account recovery process, sign out of Facebook sessions or revoke app permissions through your Facebook account's settings. Deleting a local file alone does not necessarily revoke a token or cookie.

3. Meta account settings

If the application appears in Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites, you may remove its authorization. This stops further authorized access through that integration, subject to Facebook's behavior, but does not delete data saved separately on the local device.

4. Limits

The operator cannot delete data held by Facebook or another independent platform on your behalf solely through this webpage. Requests relating to Facebook's own systems should be directed through Facebook's privacy tools. For questions email supprot@legendsmmpanel.com.
